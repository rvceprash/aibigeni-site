Business model

Digital-native, cloud-first travel startup model

AIBIGENI operates as a fully online travel technology company. EasyFly.ai is delivered entirely via web and APIs – no physical locations or hardware. Monetization is driven by consumer access and B2B partners integrating the EasyFly agent into their own products. Over time, the same AI agent will power hotels, car rentals, and other travel services.

Consumer-facing experience
Web & Mobile Web

Travelers interact with EasyFly.ai in a conversational interface to search, compare, and book flights. Traditional date and form inputs are replaced by natural language.

  • Users can ask for destinations, dates, and constraints in plain English.
  • Bookings are completed end-to-end with integrated payments.
  • Future plans include premium features on a subscription basis.
B2B / platform integrations
API & Agent-to-Agent

Third-party startups and enterprises can call the EasyFly agent via APIs to complete flight search and booking workflows and receive structured results back into their own systems, with the roadmap extending to hotels, cars, and bundled itineraries.

  • Usage-based API pricing for partners.
  • Embeddable agent endpoints for travel platforms and fintech apps.
  • Cloud-native, multi-tenant architecture for global customers.
Products

AI-native travel products

Our current focus is EasyFly.ai for flights, with a clear roadmap to extend the same conversational agent to hotels, car rentals, and other key travel services – all delivered purely as cloud-hosted software.

We primarily serve individual travelers who book their own trips online, and travel or fintech companies that want to integrate an AI travel agent into their own products.

✈️ EasyFly.ai • AI Travel Assistant

Conversational flight search & booking

EasyFly.ai lets users search, compare, and book flights using natural language instead of traditional forms. A conversational agent orchestrates flight offers, pricing, and payment, and can also be exposed to other startups as a “flight booking agent” via API. The product is currently in an MVP stage with a working demo and ongoing iterations based on early user and investor feedback.

• Built with Python (FastAPI), cloud-hosted • Uses conversational AI for entity extraction & dialog • Integrated payments & booking flows
